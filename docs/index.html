Key Features

Safety and monitoring tools in one app.

🛡Safety DashboardApp status and sensor-based risk hints, not a guarantee that you are safe
🌋Earthquake AlertsUSGS earthquake feed; not an official warning or earthquake prediction. Android may delay background checks
🆘Emergency SOSOpt-in shake SOS with a cancel countdown, saved contacts and required permissions. SMS delivery is not confirmed; does not contact 112
🧭Sensor MonitoringLive sensor readings and diagnostics
