Roadmap

Netra Email - coming soon (in planning, nothing released yet)

Netra Email aane wala hai. It is an Android email app for your Gmail accounts, built privacy-first: it talks only to your mail provider, and the AI runs on your phone. It is in the planning stage. There is no release date yet.

  • Unified inbox: All four Gmail accounts in one list, with a colour dot per account. No switching between accounts.
  • On-device 2-line Hindi summaries: A short Hindi summary under each subject, made on your phone. Nothing is sent to any cloud AI. Where a phone cannot run the model, the app says Unavailable.
  • Email Bolkar Sunao: The app reads an email aloud in Hindi or English. It follows your phone's silent and Do Not Disturb settings.
  • OTP Privacy Guard: Verification-code mails go to a separate secure section. Screenshots are blocked, codes stay hidden until you tap, and they auto-hide.
  • Tracker-pixel blocking: Remote images are blocked by default, so senders cannot see that you opened a mail. You can load images once per mail.
  • Hinglish smart replies: Short reply suggestions in your own Hindi-English mix, generated on the phone.
  • Data-light mode: On a slow or metered network, mail loads as text only. Attachments download on WiFi.
  • Automatic AI greeting (always on): A new sender gets one personalised greeting per day, written to match their mail. Never on replies inside an existing conversation. Inside your business hours you get a personalised reply; after hours, your away message.
  • Safe-reply rules: No-reply, bank, OTP, promotional and mailing-list mails never get a reply, and mail loops are blocked. Replies come in the sender's language (Hindi mail, Hindi reply).
  • Morning Hindi voice digest: A spoken Hindi summary of overnight mail at the time you choose.
  • Follow-up reminders: If a mail you sent gets no answer in 2 days, the app reminds you.
  • Indian scam shield: Rules for KYC-update, bank-block, electricity-bill and similar scams show a bold Hindi warning. It is a warning, not a guarantee.
  • Sender diary: For each contact: history, your last reply and pending items, kept on the phone.
  • Offline queue: Write mail without network; it sends when the network returns.
  • Attachment vault: PDFs and invoices sorted by sender, on the phone.
  • Privacy scorecard: A monthly report of trackers blocked, made from real counts on your phone.
  • Active Tracker: Finds receipt and application numbers in invoice mails. Where a public status page exists and you allow it, the live status shows above the mail. Where it does not, only the number is saved. A status is never invented.

Status: Planned. Every feature above is a plan, not a released function. Features that a phone cannot support will show Unavailable.

Planned items only appear when they are set by hand. Nothing here is a promise of a date.
